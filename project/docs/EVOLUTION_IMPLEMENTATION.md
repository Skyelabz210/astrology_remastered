## Verification

The native suite includes 229 new assertions covering provider consent, credential/proxy boundaries, structured citations, all 108 nakshatra/pada boundaries, Vimshottari continuity/balance, reference calendars, admitted geometry, independently checked western Vertex azimuths and eclipse catalogue properties. `types:check` checks all 21 declarations and compile-time misuse fixtures. `eclipses:check` reproduces the complete catalogue. Browser suites exercise onboarding, private notes, real computation, keepsake downloads, ZIP contents, Mistral switching and grounding repairs with mocked network responses. Final run results are recorded in the PR and repository README claim banner.

## Remaining source-dependent and research work

Prime Resonance's public sources were located and its three geometry/depth views adapted; a pixel-identical full-screen design port remains separate. No matching Celestial Registry repository was found in the connected account search. Full word-level seek/scrub and acoustic playback on physical devices require further implementation and browser/device checks. Official school-specific ayanamsha models and independent astronomical fixtures would replace the disclosed approximation. Provider latency, availability, voice entitlement and real account authentication require live credentials and cannot be established by mocked tests. None of these gaps is converted into a claim of psychological accuracy, proof of fate, or universal tradition coverage.
